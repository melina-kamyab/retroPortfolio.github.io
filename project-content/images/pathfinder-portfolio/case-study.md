# Pathfinder — making a complex product easier to understand

## Project card

**Category:** Landing-page redesign · UX/UI design

**Summary:** Redesigning Pathfinder’s landing page to explain electric transport planning and give visitors clear routes to explore the platform, start a trial, or contact the team.

## Introduction

Pathfinder helps transport businesses explore the move to electric heavy-duty vehicles. I redesigned its landing page, bringing the product story, visual hierarchy, and calls to action into a cohesive experience.

## My role

I designed the landing page, shaping its layout and the way information is presented. My focus was translating a complex offering into a page visitors could scan, understand, and explore.

## The design challenge

Electric transport planning involves several connected questions: which routes can be electrified, what charging is needed, and how costs and emissions compare. The design needed to introduce the offering without overwhelming visitors, while making the next step easy to find.

## The design approach

### Lead with the value

The hero introduces the product’s purpose and connects it to practical considerations: cost, lead time, and CO₂. A primary trial action sits alongside a secondary option for visitors who want to learn more first.

*Suggested image: pathfinder-hero.webp*

### Explain the product in manageable steps

The three-step section breaks the offering into adding data, comparing alternatives, and exporting a plan. Each card pairs a short explanation with an opportunity to explore the relevant functionality.

*Suggested image: pathfinder-how-it-works.webp*

### Support different levels of readiness

The page offers several next steps, including exploring the platform, starting a trial, and contacting the team. These options accommodate visitors who are still learning as well as those ready to engage.

*Suggested image: pathfinder-pricing.webp*

## Delivered design

The redesign presents the product through a structured landing page combining an introductory message, product explanations, customer examples, and commercial information. It demonstrates my work in interface design and in organizing a complex product story for the web.
