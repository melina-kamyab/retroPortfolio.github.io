Emissions reporting · 2022–2025

Elain

An emissions-reporting platform for heavy-transport data. My work connected UX process, frontend development, and team facilitation as the product evolved.

My roles
Frontend Developer · Scrum Master
Focus
UX process · Registration flow · Shared UI components

The product

Bring transport emissions information into one reporting experience.

Elain measures CO₂ emissions from heavy-truck transportation using vehicle, order, and fuel-provider data. The interface includes an internal admin platform for managing product data.

My contribution

Connect research, interface work, and delivery.

I introduced a UX process spanning research, wireframing, and user testing, with defined team roles and review checkpoints. I also developed the registration flow, contributed reusable components to a shared UI library, and facilitated Scrum ceremonies.

Designed around different perspectives

Personas helped make user needs visible.

The supplied personas represent different roles involved in transport and emissions reporting.
