Landing page redesign · April 2026

Pathfinder

Making electric transport planning easier to understand, with a landing page that guides visitors from the product story to a useful next step.

My contribution
Landing-page redesign
Focus
Product story · Information hierarchy · Calls to action

The challenge

Explain a connected offer without overwhelming people. Electric transport planning brings together questions about routes, charging, costs, and emissions. The page needed to introduce Pathfinder clearly while helping visitors choose how to explore the platform.

My approach

I first looked for visual noise and information competing for attention. I removed images that didn’t help explain the product, shifted the page from a dark to a lighter theme, and refined the content hierarchy so the most useful information stood out. The result was a page that was easier to scan.
