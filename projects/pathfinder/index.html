Landing page redesign · April 2026

Pathfinder

Making electric transport planning easier to understand, with a landing page that guides visitors from the product story to a useful next step.

My contribution
Landing-page design
Focus
Product story · Information hierarchy · Calls to action

The challenge

Explain a connected offer without overwhelming people.

Electric transport planning brings together questions about routes, charging, costs, and emissions. The page needed to introduce Pathfinder clearly while helping visitors choose how to explore the platform.

My approach

Build a clear path through the information.

I designed the landing page layout and information presentation around a concise introduction, a step-by-step explanation, supporting customer examples, and clear routes to learn more, start a trial, or get in touch.
